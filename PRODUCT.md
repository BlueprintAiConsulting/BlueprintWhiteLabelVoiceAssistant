# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Internal team members, technicians, and call operators at Lunar Heating & Cooling managing AI receptionists and lead routing.

## Product Purpose
To manage, monitor, and simulate the AI Voice Receptionist for an HVAC business. It captures leads, simulates caller scenarios, and provides an admin interface for tuning the AI's behavior.

## Brand Personality
High-tech, Sleek, Futuristic. The aesthetic should feel like a premium, advanced technology platform while maintaining the operational clarity required for a business tool.

## Anti-references
No specific anti-references, but avoiding generic SaaS aesthetics (flat gray/white) in favor of a sleeker, more futuristic presentation.

## Design Principles
- **Expert Confidence:** The UI should exude advanced technical capability without feeling chaotic.
- **Signal Over Noise:** Operations tools need high legibility; futuristic styling shouldn't compromise readability.
- **Immediate Feedback:** Interactions (like live voice calls) should have tangible, real-time visual feedback (e.g. glowing pulses, clear active states).

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Ensure high contrast for text over dark or futuristic backgrounds (minimum WCAG AA 4.5:1). Clear focus states for form inputs and simulator controls.
